### What I actually do

I design and run the infrastructure other people take for granted — the
network under a stadium on match day, the hypervisors under the line-of-business
apps, the backups that have to work the one time they are needed. Lately most of
that is **self-hosted AI on my own metal** and **zero-trust networking**, because
both turn out to be infrastructure problems rather than product ones.

- 🔥 **Next-generation firewalls at the perimeter** — an HA firewall pair doing policy-based routing, IPS and application control with site-to-site IPsec and SSL VPN, and open-source firewalls where I want the rules, the IDS and the logs to be mine: community threat intelligence and intrusion detection inline, the whole estate segmented behind both
- 🛰️ **Wireless that holds under load** — VLAN-segmented, controller-managed, 10,000+ concurrent clients on match day without a support queue
- 🧭 **Routing and switching by hand** — enterprise routers, switches and access points: VLANs, OSPF, VRRP, QoS that survives a full stadium, a validating DNS resolver with DNSSEC
- 🐾 **Alfa and her squad** — my own agents, on my own models and my own hardware. **Alfa** is the catgirl assistant I actually talk to all day, in chat, across the estate; she runs a squad of specialists, and **Alfa Cody** is the techie of the group — he lives in the repositories
- 🔐 **Zero trust, in practice** — modern VPN tunnels instead of a flat LAN, per-device policy, nothing trusted because of where it plugged in
- 💾 **Disaster recovery that is tested** — multi-site off-site replication on self-hosted storage
- 🧠 **Building with Alfa Cody, hands on the wheel** — I decide the structure, the data model and what "done" means; Cody writes the passes and I review every one
- 📦 **Open source** — [NoCapOS](https://github.com/shatheitguy/nocapos), a home server OS that turns any PC or Raspberry Pi into your own cloud, [IT-Vault](https://github.com/shatheitguy/it-vault), an asset register and helpdesk for whoever *is* the IT department, [FormCraft](https://github.com/shatheitguy/formcraft), a self-hosted form builder that speaks English, Arabic and Tamil, and [ALFA Launcher](https://github.com/shatheitguy/alfa-launcher), the Android home screen I wanted on my own phone, and [ZeroLeakage](https://github.com/shatheitguy/ZeroLeakage), a privacy shield for Windows and Android that stops your devices talking behind your back — with the templates for my containers in [one repository](https://github.com/shatheitguy/unraid-templates)

**[ZeroLeakage](https://github.com/shatheitguy/ZeroLeakage)** · Windows + Android

*Zero bits leaked.* A privacy shield that cuts telemetry, trackers and unknown
apps off the network. On Android it is a real on-device DNS firewall: a local
VPN that routes only DNS, names the app behind every lookup, filters it through
AdGuard-style lists (OISD, HaGeZi, StevenBlack and more) and resolves the rest
over DNS-over-HTTPS with DNSSEC, with per-app kill switches, a Strict Guard
"ask to connect" mode and a packet inspector down to the country each IP lives
in. Or skip the VPN entirely with Lite mode on Android's Private DNS. On Windows:
a dark-glass command centre that starts with the PC, lives in the tray and scans
through Microsoft Defender.

`Kotlin` `Jetpack Compose` `C#` `WPF` `.NET` `WiX`

### How I build

I ship software the same way I ship infrastructure: decide the shape first,
then move quickly and check the result. Knowing the structure is the part that
does not delegate — the data model, where state lives, what breaks under load,
what "finished" actually means. The typing delegates fine.

So I build with an agent in the loop and my hands on the wheel — mostly
**Alfa Cody**, the techie of Alfa's squad. They are mine: my own models, my
own hardware, reachable from my phone. Alfa runs the day; Cody lives in the
repositories. I specify and review, Cody writes the passes, and nothing lands
that I have not read.

It is how a one-person IT department ships a web app, an Android client, a
one-line installer and a test suite in the same month — and why every commit
message in [IT-Vault](https://github.com/shatheitguy/it-vault) explains *why*,
not just what.
